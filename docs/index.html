      Quick reference handbook

      The questions people ask before they install.

      Is it really free?

      Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no “pro” version.

      Will it slow down my games?

      No. Game Mode boosts your game and Compubase itself goes ultra-light while you play: wallpaper, widgets and animations pause and it hands its memory back to Windows. Hotkeys keep working.

      What does Windows' blue warning mean?

      “Windows protected your PC” appears for new apps that few people have downloaded yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

      Will it break my taskbar?

      No. The Compu Dock only hides the Windows taskbar. Switching the dock off, quitting, uninstalling, or even a crash brings the normal taskbar straight back.

      I had NexusHub. Now what?

      NexusHub is now Compubase. Your copy offers the update itself (or install from this page); settings, profiles and scores move over and the old version is removed.

      How do updates work?

      Compubase checks once a day and shows Update now on Home. It updates and restarts in about a minute, keeping your settings.

      Does it work in fullscreen games?

      Hotkeys do. The crosshair and stats overlay work in borderless and windowed modes. Check your game's rules before using overlays in ranked matches.

      How do I uninstall it?

      Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Compubase → Uninstall. You can choose to delete your settings too.

      What does the leaderboard share?

      Only the nickname you choose and your best score, and you can switch it off. Games on this website stay on your device.

      Where are my settings stored?

      On your PC, in your user folder. Signing in with Google is optional and only backs up your profiles to a private folder in your own Drive.
