Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026

The short version: NexusHub is a Windows app that runs on your computer. We don't run servers that collect your information, there are no ads, and there is no tracking or analytics. If you choose to sign in with Google, your NexusHub profiles are saved in a private app folder in your own Google Drive so you can use them on your other PCs. That's the only thing NexusHub does with your Google account.

1. Who we are

NexusHub (“NexusHub”, “we”, “us”) is a desktop application for Microsoft Windows. You can contact us about this policy at dovajova36@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your computer

NexusHub stores its settings on your PC in %APPDATA%\NexusHub. This includes:

  • Your settings and profiles (theme, hotkeys, dock layout, favourites, and so on).
  • Clipboard history, if you switch on the Clipboard History module.
  • Speed test results, wallpaper and soundboard choices, and cached images such as album covers and website icons.
  • Cookies and site data from the built-in browser.
  • A log file used to fix errors.

We never receive this information. It stays on your computer unless you delete it.

3. Google sign-in (optional)

Signing in with Google is optional. NexusHub works fully without it. If you do sign in, NexusHub asks Google for the following permissions:

PermissionWhat NexusHub uses it for
Your name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile) To show which account you're signed in with in NexusHub, and to keep each account's profiles separate on your PC.
See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata) To save and sync your NexusHub profiles in Drive's hidden application-data folder. This folder is private to NexusHub. NexusHub cannot see, open or change any of your other Google Drive files, and the folder doesn't appear in your Drive's file list.

What NexusHub saves in your Google Drive

A list of your profile names and one file per profile containing your NexusHub settings. Before uploading, NexusHub removes private items: your Spotify login, your clipboard history and your built-in browser data are never uploaded.

How your Google sign-in is stored

The long-term sign-in token Google gives NexusHub is stored only on your PC, encrypted with Windows Data Protection, so only your Windows user account can read it. Your name, email and profile picture are stored on your PC so NexusHub can display them.

Limited use of Google user data

NexusHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the sign-in and profile-sync features described above.
  • We do not sell Google user data, and do not transfer it to anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads your Google user data. The only exception would be if you explicitly ask us to help with a problem and share it with us yourself, or if the law requires it.

The Flappy Nexus leaderboard

If you play Flappy Nexus with “Send my best scores to the leaderboard” switched on (you can switch it off in the game's options), your leaderboard name and best score are shown publicly on this website and in NexusHub. Pick a nickname if you don't want to use your real name. The anonymous player ID isn't linked to your Google account, your email or your PC's name. Games you play on this website are not sent anywhere; your best web score is kept only in your own browser.

5. What we don't do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no selling of data.
  • No NexusHub servers that store your information. The only thing stored online is the optional game leaderboard described above (a nickname and a score).

6. Deleting your data

  • Sign out: NexusHub → Account → Sign out. This deletes the sign-in token from your PC.
  • Remove NexusHub's access to your Google account: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose NexusHub, and remove access.
  • Delete the profiles saved in your Drive: delete them in NexusHub (Account → Profiles), or open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → NexusHub → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove your leaderboard entry: email us your leaderboard name and we'll delete it.
  • Delete everything on your PC: quit NexusHub and delete the folder %APPDATA%\NexusHub.

7. Security

Sign-in uses Google's official OAuth flow in your own web browser, with PKCE. NexusHub never sees your Google password. Connections to Google and other services use HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are encrypted on your PC with Windows Data Protection.

8. Children

NexusHub is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be announced on this website.
